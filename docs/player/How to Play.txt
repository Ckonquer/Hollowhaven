HOLLOWHAVEN - For Krystal
=========================

To play: double-click  Hollowhaven.exe
No installation, no account, no internet needed. Your progress is saved in
  %APPDATA%\Hollowhaven\saves   (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to find it)

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (the game isn't code-signed):
  click "More info", then "Run anyway". This only appears the first time.

THE STORY
The old Ward Stone in the clearing is dimming. Make the clearing your home, befriend the
little creatures of the woods, prepare - and bring back the light from deep below the old mine.
Follow the goal shown at the top right of the screen. Press J for your journal, M for the map.

CONTROLS (change them any time in Settings > Controls)
  Move ..................... W A S D          Look ............ Mouse
  Jump ..................... Space            Sprint .......... Shift
  Dodge roll ............... Ctrl (or V)      Camera zoom ..... Mouse wheel
  Attack / chop / mine ..... Left mouse       Eat / use item .. Right mouse (or Q)
  Interact / pick up ....... E                Lantern ......... F
  Inventory ................ Tab (or I)       Crafting ........ C
  Build mode ............... B  (R rotate, X take down, Left mouse place)
  Journal .................. J                Map ............. M
  Companion follow / wait .. G                Hotbar .......... 1 - 8
  Pause / menu ............. Esc              All controls .... F1
  Screenshot ............... F12  (saved to %APPDATA%\Hollowhaven\screenshots)

COMFORT SETTINGS (Pause > Settings > Comfort)
  Horror intensity: Gentle / Standard / Intense - the underground can be scary.
  Arachnophobia mode: replaces every spider-like creature, web, sound and icon with a
  non-spider alternative. Also: reduce camera shake, reduce flashing, sound captions,
  and separate volume sliders for music, ambience, effects and menus.

TIPS
  - Sleeping in a bed saves your game and sets where you wake up.
  - Underground, light the lanterns on the walls to save your progress.
  - Enemies always wiggle, flare or rattle before they attack - that's your cue to dodge.
  - If you ever get stuck, open the pause menu and choose "Return to the Haven".
  - Nothing you build or befriend can be lost. Take your time.

Made with love, for Krystal.

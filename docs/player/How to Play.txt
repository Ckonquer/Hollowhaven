HOLLOWHAVEN - How to Play
=========================
Made with love, for Krystal.

Hollowhaven is a small, cozy 3D adventure. Make an old clearing in the woods your home, befriend
the shy little creatures who live there, build and grow things - and, when you feel ready, bring
the light back from deep below the old mine. It is gentle almost everywhere. Only the deep places
underground are meant to feel a little spooky, and you choose how spooky (see COMFORT below).
No account and no internet needed.

STARTING
  Open Hollowhaven from the Start menu or from its shortcut on your Desktop. (This page is in
  the Start menu too, as "How to Play".)
  Choose "New Game".
  1. "Before You Begin": the comfort options (see COMFORT below). Pick what feels right, then
     "Begin the journey".
  2. "Meet Krystal": choose her hair, colours, eyes and outfit - it's all just for looks, and you
     can change everything later. Then "Confirm".
  Your journey is saved straight away. From then on the title screen also shows "Continue", with
  the day and your current goal: press it to carry on where you left off.
  If Windows ever says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway"
  (Hollowhaven is homemade and not code-signed).

FINDING YOUR WAY
  Your current goal is shown at the top right of the screen.
  J opens your Journal: the story so far, optional goals, your friends, notes you have found
  and your masteries. M opens the map.
  A friendly tip pops up the first time you meet something new. F1 shows every control.

CONTROLS (most keys can be changed in Settings > Controls)
  Move ..................... W A S D (or arrows)   Look around ........ Mouse
  Jump ..................... Space                 Sprint ............. Shift (hold)
  Dodge roll ............... Ctrl (or V)           Camera zoom ........ Mouse wheel
  Attack / chop / mine ..... Left mouse            Eat / use item ..... Right mouse (or Q)
  Interact / pick up ....... E                     Raise lantern ...... F
  Backpack ................. Tab (or I)            Crafting ........... C
  Build mode ............... B                     Wardrobe ........... O
  Journal .................. J                     Map ................ M
  Companion follow / wait .. G                     Companion skill .... T
  Talk to a friend ......... E (on the friend)     Hotbar slot ........ 1 - 8
  Pause menu ............... Esc                   All controls ....... F1
  Screenshot ............... F12  (saved in %APPDATA%\Hollowhaven\screenshots)
  Left mouse picks your best axe, pickaxe or weapon by itself - no need to switch. To eat, pick
  the food on your hotbar (1 - 8) and press Right mouse. Armor and charms: open your backpack
  (Tab) and drag them onto the Equipped column.

SAVING
  - Your journey is saved when you start it, every time you go from one area to another (for
    example down into the old mine, or back up), and when you sleep in a bed you have built
    (sleeping also sets where you wake up).
  - In the woods (and later in Hallowmere Reach) the game also saves by itself every few minutes
    and when you close the window, and Pause (Esc) > Save Game works whenever nothing is chasing
    you and you're not in a big fight.
  - In the old mine and the deep places below it, light the lanterns on the walls (E): each one
    saves your progress and becomes the place you return to. A few other places let you save
    only while you rest somewhere safe, such as by a lit lantern.
  - "Quit to Title" and "Quit Game" in the pause menu offer "Save & Quit" whenever saving is
    possible there.
  - If Krystal is defeated, she wakes up somewhere safe with everything she was carrying.

FRIENDS
  Shy little creatures live in the woods. Be gentle and patient with them - the game shows you
  how - and they may become your friends.
  - A friend follows you and helps in its own way. G asks it to wait; press G nearby to have it
    follow you again. Press E on a friend for its menu: how it helps in fights, its special
    skill, a home spot and more.
  - Friends help in fights, and each one has a special skill (a heal, a shield or a burst of
    speed). They use it by themselves when it helps, or press T. The ring round their portrait
    (top left) shows when it's ready again.
  - Mochi is too timid to follow you underground at first. Journal > Optional shows how to
    help it feel brave.

BUILDING YOUR HOME
  Gather wood, stone and plant fiber, then open Crafting (C) to make tools and a Workbench.
  Many recipes need you to stand near your Workbench.
  Press B for build mode (you can build at the Haven):
    Left mouse ....... place a piece           R ... rotate
    X ................ take a piece down (you get everything back)
    Z ................ undo your last piece (press twice)
    P ................ recolour a piece with dyes from the Loom
    N ................ choose a carved pumpkin's face
    Mouse wheel or 1 - 8 ... choose a piece    B or Esc ... finish building
  Stairs lead up to an upper floor, held up by walls, windows, doorways or porch posts.
  Crops grow in a Crop Plot as time passes (look at the plot and press E to plant seeds), and a
  friend at home tends them so they grow faster.
  Crafting tip: tick "Also use nearby chests" in the crafting menu to use what's in your chests.

THE WARDROBE
  Press O (or Pause > Wardrobe) to change Krystal's hair, colours, eyes and clothes any time.
  It's free and only for looks: her armor still protects her underneath (tick "Show equipped
  armor" if you'd like to see it). Three "Saved looks" slots keep your favourite outfits.
  You can also build a Wardrobe or a Vanity Mirror at the Workbench and press E at it.
  More clothes are sewn from patterns at the Loom, and some are found along the way.

CHAPTER TWO
  Hollowhaven has two chapters in one journey. When Chapter One is finished you simply keep
  playing in the same world, and the goal box at the top right says "The Ward Stone has
  something new to say". Walk up to the Ward Stone in your Haven and press E.
  "Follow the lantern road" begins Chapter Two, "The Lanterns of Hallowmere": a road to an
  autumn town with a festival waiting to be finished. "Stay a little longer" changes nothing -
  the Stone asks again whenever you come back to it.
  Your house, your friends and everything you have made come along. In Hallowmere Reach, the
  waygate where you arrive always leads home, and any lit lantern arch can take you back to the
  Haven along the Lantern Road.

COMFORT
  Change these any time: Pause (Esc) > Comfort & Accessibility, or Settings > Comfort.
  They also come up once more just before you first go down into the old mine.
  - Horror intensity: Gentle, Standard or Intense. Gentle means fewer scares, no ambushes,
    softer sounds and enemies that hit less hard. The surface is always cozy.
  - Arachnophobia mode: turns everything spider-like in the game into something that isn't -
    the creatures, the webs, the sounds and the icons. Switch it on before you start if you'd
    rather not see spiders at all.
  - Reduce camera shake, Reduce flashing, and Captions (short text for important sounds).
  - Settings > Comfort also has High-visibility cues (bolder outlines and markers, so nothing
    depends on colour alone) and Timing assist (longer warnings and roomier timing in puzzles
    and boss fights - nothing is skipped).
  - Settings > Audio: separate volumes for everything (master), music, ambience, sound effects
    and the interface.
  - Settings > Gameplay: "Show hints", "Friends join fights", "Friends use skills on their
    own", "Seasonal decorations" (a few pumpkins and ribbons along the woodland paths - it
    starts off), and "Show tips again" while you're playing.

IF THE GAME RUNS SLOWLY
  Settings > Video: set "Graphics quality" to Medium or Low, or lower "Render scale" (text and
  menus stay sharp). "Frame rate limit" keeps a laptop cooler and quieter, and "Fullscreen" can
  be switched off to play in a window. Settings is on the title screen and in the pause menu.

IF YOU EVER GET STUCK
  Open the pause menu (Esc) and choose "Return to the Haven". In the old mine and the deep
  places below it, it says "Return to last lantern"; inside an Echo Trial, "Leave the trial";
  and anywhere else, "Return to safety".

LITTLE TIPS
  - Enemies give a little sign before they attack - a wiggle, a flare, a rattle. That's your
    cue to roll away (Ctrl).
  - It's dark underground. The story has you craft a Lantern before you go down: press F to
    raise it. Many things down there shrink from its light.
  - Once a great fight is won, a pale echo stone waits nearby. Press E there to replay that
    fight as a memory, for a small reward. Nothing on your journey changes.
  - When the story is over, free play simply carries on in the same save, with a few new
    things to make and places to wander.
  - There is no rush. Take your time.

WHERE YOUR SAVES LIVE
  %APPDATA%\Hollowhaven\saves   (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to open it)
  Uninstalling Hollowhaven (Windows Settings > Apps) keeps your saves and settings, so if you
  ever install it again, just press "Continue".

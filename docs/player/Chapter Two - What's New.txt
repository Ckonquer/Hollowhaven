HOLLOWHAVEN - Chapter Two: The Lanterns of Hallowmere
What's new
=====================================================

A second chapter for your haven. Everything you made is still there, and a lantern road
now leads somewhere new - whenever you feel like following it.

PRESS "CONTINUE", NOT "NEW GAME"
  Continue opens your own save: the same house, furniture, chests, crops, friends and
  finished story. New Game would start a fresh journey over it (it asks first).
  The first time this version saves, it tucks an untouched copy of your Chapter One save
  into  %APPDATA%\Hollowhaven\saves\pre_v2_backup  . The older copy of the game can't read
  the new save, so keep playing with this one.

STYLE KRYSTAL
  Soon after you Continue, somewhere calm, a little card says "Your wardrobe is ready".
  Choose "Open the wardrobe" to change her hair, colours, eyes, outfits and single pieces.
  Later, any time: press O (the Wardrobe key), or Pause (Esc) > Wardrobe, or build a
  Wardrobe or Vanity Mirror at the Workbench and press E at it. The Ward Stone and a
  festival mirror in the Lanternwake Commons offer "Change your look" too, once the new
  road is open.
  Three preset slots keep favourite looks. Clothes are only for looks: armor still protects
  her underneath (tick "Show equipped armor" in the wardrobe if you'd like to see it).
  New outfits are sewn as patterns at the Loom, and some are found along the way.

YOUR FRIENDS FIGHT BESIDE YOU
  Mochi, Pebble and Lumi now help in fights, and each has a special skill: Mochi's
  Bloomguard heals, Pebble's Stoneheart shields, Lumi's Glimmerstep quickens your step.
  They use it when it helps, or press T (companion skill). The ring round their portrait
  shows when it's ready again.
  Press E on a friend for their menu: Assist or Stay close, the skill, "Attacks on its own",
  "Uses its skill on its own", and a home spot. G still asks them to follow or wait.
  Mochi used to stop at the mine. Tie a Comfort Ribbon (sewn at the Loom) from Mochi's menu,
  then rest together by the lit lantern at the mine's mouth, and Mochi will come below too.
  A Companion Bed or Perch at home gives a friend a spot of its own (press E on it).

WHERE THE NEW STORY BEGINS
  With Chapter One finished, the goal box at the top right says "The Ward Stone has
  something new to say". Read the Ward Stone in your Haven (E). "Follow the lantern road"
  begins Chapter Two; "Stay a little longer" changes nothing, and it will ask again next time.
  The road starts at an old waygate north of the Moonlit Ruin: mend it with 8 wood,
  4 stone and 2 copper ore, and step through to Hallowmere Reach, a town that never finished
  its lantern festival.

GOING HOME
  The waygate where you arrive in the Reach always leads straight back to the woods.
  Light the lantern arches you find there: at any lit arch, "Travel the Lantern Road" takes
  you to another arch or home to the Haven, and from home the Ward Stone sends you back.
  In the woods, Pause > "Return to the Haven" works as before. Your house stays exactly as
  you built it; the story never moves or adds anything inside it.

NEW GEAR
  A Furnace (Workbench) turns ore into bars. Queue a few batches; they smelt while you play.
  Armor for head, body and boots, plus charms: open the backpack (Tab) and drag them onto
  the Equipped column. Stronger axes, pickaxes and swords, and a Sparkseed Wand.
  The Loom & Dye Bench (a tailor's cache near the Old Oak shows you how) weaves cloth,
  sews clothes, mixes dyes and makes a bigger backpack (40, then 48 slots). A storage chest
  can grow to 40 slots from its own menu, and crafting can "Also use nearby chests".
  Masteries (Journal > Mastery) grow quietly with gathering, crafting and exploring.

BUILDING
  The Haven's building ground reaches further now, with lawns to the west and south-west.
  New: stairs and an upper floor (held up by walls, windows, doorways or porch posts),
  railings, half walls, arched windows, gable roofs, flower boxes, curtains, bookshelves,
  a kitchen counter, a cottage bench, a music box and more.
  In build mode (B): Z takes back your last piece (press twice, full refund), P recolours
  the piece you point at with dyes from the Loom, N picks a carved pumpkin's face. Like
  every other key, they can be changed in Settings > Controls.
  There is a small cottage plot by the Lanternwake Commons for building in the Reach too.

HALLOWEEN
  Hallowmere Reach is autumn all year: pumpkin orchards, candlelit paths and a very
  theatrical manor. Grow pumpkins in a Crop Plot, cook festival food at the new Kitchen
  Counter, and make carved pumpkins, pumpkin lights, bat bunting, ghost lanterns, a
  scarecrow and more for your home as their recipes turn up.
  Settings > Gameplay > "Seasonal decorations" adds a few pumpkins and ribbons along the
  woodland paths (never inside your home). It starts off.

MUSIC
  Lots of new music for both chapters: home by day and by night, the woods, the old mine,
  the Reach's orchard, quarry and candleyard, the manor, fights and the celebration. The old
  favourites are still there. Settings > Audio > Music sets the volume, and there is a
  Music Box to build for your home (see below).

COMFORT
  Arachnophobia mode covers the new creatures too. New in Settings > Comfort:
  "High-visibility cues" and "Timing assist". In Settings > Gameplay: "Friends join fights"
  and "Friends use skills on their own".

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Echo Trials: once a great fight is won, a pale echo stone waits by the lantern (or the
    candles) before it - by the Heartseed Chamber, in the manor ballroom and at the
    Bell Throat. Press E to replay that fight as a memory. The first clear of each teaches
    a new outfit pattern for the Loom; later clears leave a few materials, waiting for you
    back at the stone. Nothing on your journey changes, and you can leave by the pale door.
  - After the story ends, free play simply carries on in the same save. A new Workbench
    recipe, the Expedition Board, opens three hand-made Lanternwake Expeditions through
    the Reach (Orchard Patrol, Quarry Circuit, Manor Afterglow): a few stops, then a cache
    of materials and treats, sometimes a keepsake. No timers; setting one aside is free.
  - The Music Box (Workbench) plays the songs you have heard in calm places. Press E at
    it, pick a song, and mark favourites: they come round a little more often at home.
    Fights and scary moments still keep their own music.
  - New tips appear once each. Settings > Gameplay > "Show tips again" brings them back.
  - One small thing: the older version of the game can't open the new save (the backup
    above is for that).
  - All controls: F1. Full guide: How to Play.txt.
